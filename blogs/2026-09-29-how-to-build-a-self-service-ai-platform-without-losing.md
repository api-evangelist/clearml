By Adam Wolf Most enterprise AI infrastructure conversations land on the same tension. Builders want self-service: instant access to GPUs, a stable endpoint for a model, a notebook, or a fine-tuning run that starts without a ticket. Platform teams want per-team quotas, identity-aware access, audit trails, tenant isolation, and cost attribution they can defend.
